**Dusk Launcher** is a minimalist Android launcher designed to eliminate visual distractions, reduce screen time, and maximize daily focus. By replacing complex interfaces with a clean, typography-driven layout, Dusk transforms your device into a streamlined and highly responsive tool.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🌿 Pure Minimalism:** No cluttered icon grids or intrusive widgets. Enjoy a clean text-based list, custom backgrounds, and a lightning-fast search bar to keep your workspace intentional.
* **⚡ Engineered for Speed:** Lightweight architecture ensures fluid and snappy performance, even on older or entry-level Android devices.
* **🔋 Battery Efficient:** Minimal background processes and a resource-conscious design drastically reduce battery consumption compared to traditional launchers.
* **🛠️ Essential Utilities:** Quick access to core system tools—such as Phone, Messages, Camera, and Flashlight—directly from a clean bottom navigation dock.
* **🌗 Native Light & Dark Themes:** Seamless support for both light and dark modes to complement your visual preferences and lighting conditions.
